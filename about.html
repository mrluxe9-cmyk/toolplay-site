About ToolPlay

ToolPlay brings together everyday calculators, converters and utilities alongside a handful of quick browser games — all free, all working directly in your browser, with no account required.

What we believe

Every tool should actually do the job it says it does, and every game should actually be playable — not a mockup. That's the bar for anything we add.

Your data

Favorites and recently-used items are stored locally in your browser using localStorage — we don't require an account to use any tool or game.
