How this password generator works

Passwords are generated entirely in your browser using the Web Crypto API's cryptographically secure random number generator — nothing is sent to a server.

FAQ

How long should my password be?

Most security guidance recommends at least 12–16 characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols.
