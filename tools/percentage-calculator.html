What is a percentage calculator?

A percentage calculator handles the three most common percent problems: finding a share of a number, expressing one number as a percentage of another, and finding the percent change between two values.

Example

20% of 150 is 30. 30 is 20% of 150. Going from 150 to 180 is a 20% increase.
