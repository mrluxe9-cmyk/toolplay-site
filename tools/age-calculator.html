Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days.

What is an age calculator?

An age calculator works out the exact time between a birth date and a reference date (today, by default), broken down into years, months and days rather than a single rounded number.

How it works

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it to today's date (or any date you choose) and works out the full years, remaining months, and remaining days between the two — plus your total age in weeks and days.

FAQ

Does it account for leap years?

Yes — the calculation is based on actual calendar dates, so leap years are handled automatically.

Can I calculate age as of a future or past date?

Yes, use the "Calculate age as of" field to pick any reference date.
