What is a word counter?

A word counter analyzes text as you type, giving instant counts of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs — useful for essays, social posts, and anywhere with a length limit.

How reading time is calculated

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, a common average adult silent reading speed.
