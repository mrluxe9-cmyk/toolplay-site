Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

Local storage

ToolPlay stores your theme preference, favorites, and recently-used items in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage (e.g. which tools are popular). This is configured in a single analytics config file and can be disabled by blocking analytics scripts in your browser.

Advertising

Reserved ad placements may display third-party ads (e.g. Google AdSense) once configured. Ad networks may use cookies as described in their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our contact page.
