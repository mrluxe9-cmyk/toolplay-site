Tic Tac Toe

Classic 2-player X and O — play on one device, take turns.

How to play

  • Players take turns placing X or O on the 3×3 grid.
  • First to get three in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins.
  • If all 9 squares fill with no winner, it's a draw.
